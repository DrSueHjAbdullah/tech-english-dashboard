Grammar

Passive Voice in Technical Writing

Used to focus on the process rather than the agent. E.g., "The data was collected using sensors."

Vocabulary

Key Technical Verbs

analyse, implement, configure, integrate, validate, optimise, deploy, simulate.

Writing

Abstract Structure

Background → Objective → Method → Results → Conclusion. Keep under 250 words.

Pronunciation

Stress in Technical Terms

SYS-tem, pro-CESS, CON-text, a-NAL-y-sis — stress shifts often when adding suffixes.

Lesson Reference
Unit 5 · Technical Reports — focus on impersonal language, hedging expressions (it appears that, this suggests), and clear paragraph signposting. Next class: Thursday, 10:00 AM — Lab 204 🏫
